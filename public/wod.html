Questions

Frequently asked questions

Are these hybrid race workouts?

Yes. They are built for hybrid race training: runs plus the race stations (SkiErg, sled push and pull, burpee broad jumps, row, farmers carry, sandbag lunges and wall balls) at race weights. Hybrid Plan Builder is independent and not affiliated with or endorsed by any race organiser.

How long is each workout?

They average 45 minutes of main work. Add a 10-minute warm-up and a 5-minute cool-down.

Do I need a partner?

No. Every workout has a solo version and a team-of-2 version. Pick the one that suits you that day.

What equipment do I need?

A gym with the usual race equipment is ideal: rower, SkiErg, sled, kettlebells, sandbag and wall ball. Missing something? Every piece of equipment has an alternative, such as rowing the same metres when there is no SkiErg.

Can I see the workouts ahead of time?

Yes. The whole week, Monday to Sunday, opens on Sunday at 6 pm your time, so you can plan your sessions.

Do I have to record my scores?

No. Each workout says how it is scored, so you can track it in your own notes if you like.

Can I cancel?

Yes, any time, from Account › Manage subscription. The Workout of the Day is $5 a month.
