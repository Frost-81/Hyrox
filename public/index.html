How the 12 weeks are built

Three phases to race day

Each phase builds on the last: first a solid base, then strength and race-specific work, then pacing and a taper so you start your race fresh.

  1. Weeks 1–4

    Building the foundation

    Get used to the training, set your paces with a 5k test, then add volume and the first harder sessions. Week 4 is lighter so your body absorbs the work.

  2. Weeks 5–8

    Strength & power

    Race-specific blocks begin: runs straight into stations, heavier strength work and station loads that build to an overload in week 7, and a half race simulation in week 6. A second 5k test closes week 8.

  3. Weeks 9–12

    Specificity & peaking

    Race pacing and a full race simulation, then a taper that cuts the volume while keeping you sharp, and a race week that gets you to the start line fresh.

A typical week

Six sessions, one rest day

Every week follows the same rhythm, so training fits around work and family. Each session comes with your paces, weights and distances filled in.

  • Mon

    Endurance. Running or machine intervals that build your engine. Easier and harder weeks alternate, and week 1 starts with a 5k test.

  • Tue

    Strength. Squats, presses, deadlifts and pulls, with target weights set from your body weight.

  • Wed

    Active recovery. About 45 minutes of easy cardio, mobility and foam rolling.

  • Thu

    Hybrid. Runs and stations back to back, written like a workout of the day, about 40–45 minutes.

  • Fri

    Skill + WOD. Station technique and strength, then one short workout of the day.

  • Sat

    Long session. A long easy run in the first weeks; 5k tests and race simulations later on.

  • Sun

    Rest. Sleep, eat well and go for a walk if you like.

Hard Moderate Easy Rest

Pick any 5 or 6 days. Your long session goes on your last training day, hard sessions are kept off back-to-back days where possible, and on 5 days the recovery session is dropped.

What it focuses on

Everything race day asks of you

Running on tired legs

Half of your race is running. Easy mileage builds the base, threshold and 5k-pace work raise your speed, and mock race blocks teach you to run well straight after a station.

Strong at every station

Sled loads start at 80% of race weight and build to a 110% overload in week 7, so race day feels lighter, then drop for the taper. Strength days keep your legs and grip ready for the sleds, carries and lunges.

An engine for the machines

SkiErg and rowing intervals come with a target split per 500 m, and the bike fills in when you need a swap. Your engine carries you through the whole race.

Pacing and recovery

Your race splits show what your goal time means for each run and station, and simulations let you rehearse the whole race. An active recovery day, a lighter week 4 and a proper taper keep you fresh.

Questions

Frequently asked questions

Is this a hybrid race training program?

Yes. It is a 12-week training program built for hybrid fitness races: 8 x 1 km runs, each followed by a station (SkiErg, sled push, sled pull, burpee broad jumps, row, farmers carry, sandbag lunges and wall balls), at the weights of your division. It is not an official program of any race: Hybrid Plan Builder is independent and not affiliated with or endorsed by any race organiser.

Who is the 12-week plan for?

Anyone training for a hybrid fitness race of 8 x 1 km runs and 8 stations, from first-timers to athletes chasing a faster time. Pick your division (Open, Pro or Doubles) and age group, and the plan sets the weights and recovery to match.

My race is less than 12 weeks away. Can I still use it?

Yes. The plan starts at the current week and leaves out the earlier ones. The first week shown includes a 5k test to set your paces, and the taper is kept in full.

My race is more than 12 weeks away. What do I do until then?

The plan shows the date your 12 weeks start and how to build your base until then.

I don't have a rower or SkiErg. Does the plan still work?

Yes. Untick the machines you don't have. Those sessions are swapped for running or another machine using a distance chart, so the amount of work stays the same.

Does it work for doubles?

Yes. Choose Men, Women or Mixed Doubles. You run every kilometre together and share the station work, but each partner should still follow the full plan.

Is it a subscription?

No. You pay $5 once for the 12-week plan. It stays unlocked on your account, on any device.

Can I change my goal or training days later?

Yes, at any time. Your plan updates straight away and your 5k test results are kept. You can also keep a plan for more than one race.

Kilograms or pounds?

Either. Switch at any time and every weight in the plan is converted.
